01 / About

A decade of watching marketing fail to prove itself. So I fixed it.

I am Charlie Barrett. Large corporates, SMEs, full-time roles and freelance projects, and everywhere the same gap between activity and results. CBC exists to close it.

02 / Why me

For the last decade I have worked inside large corporate organisations and alongside SMEs. The same frustration followed me everywhere: marketing that could not match its actions to results.

That is the problem I have set out to fix, in every full-time role and every freelance project. I work with people, not around them, to build marketing processes that actually work and connect directly to commercial activity. I understand what drives value in a business and build the system around that. No wasted effort. No wasted spend. Agreed actions that solve a real problem.

The brands I work best with know there is scope to grow but do not always have the answers to support it. That is where I come in. I help you understand what is holding you back, then I deliver the fix with you, so the capability stays in your business.

03 / How I work

Four principles I do not compromise on.

Systems over heroics

Growth that depends on one person burning out is not growth. It is a risk. I build for repeatability.

Measure what compounds

Vanity metrics are noise. I anchor every engagement to the numbers that move the business.

Advise, then install

Advice you cannot act on is worthless. I stay long enough to build the thing, not just recommend it.

Clarity before spend

Most teams do not have a budget problem. They have a focus problem. We fix that first.
