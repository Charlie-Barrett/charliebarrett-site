01 / About

A decade making marketing prove itself.

I am Charlie Barrett. Ten years inside a global manufacturer, a director role at a UK garden products manufacturer, and consultancy for industrial and technical businesses. Everywhere, the same gap between activity and results. CBC exists to close it.

02 / Why me

I spent ten years inside a global manufacturer and now work alongside founder-led industrial and technical businesses. The same frustration followed me everywhere: marketing that could not match its actions to results.

That is the problem I have set out to fix, in every full-time role and every freelance project. I work with people, not around them, to build marketing processes that actually work and connect directly to commercial activity. I understand what drives value in a business and build the system around that. No wasted effort. No wasted spend. Agreed actions that solve a real problem.

The businesses I work best with know there is scope to grow but do not always have the answers to support it. That is where I come in. I help you understand what is holding you back, then I deliver the fix with you, so the capability stays in your business.

Outside client work I have guest lectured in digital marketing at the University of Worcester, which is where the habit of explaining the why, not just the what, comes from. I live in Worcestershire with my family. If I am not at Sixways for a home game, I am probably somewhere abroad with a rucksack.

03 / How I work

Four principles I do not compromise on.

Systems over heroics

Growth that depends on one person burning out is not growth. It is a risk. I build for repeatability.

Measure what compounds

Vanity metrics are noise. I anchor every engagement to the numbers that move the business.

Advise, then install

Advice you cannot act on is worthless. I stay long enough to build the thing, not just recommend it.

Clarity before spend

Most teams do not have a budget problem. They have a focus problem. We fix that first.
