American Express referral code UK

Everything you need to know about the Amex Refer a Friend scheme in the UK — how it works for both referrers and referees, how to find your unique referral link, and what bonus Membership Rewards points you can earn.

This page contains contextual information about the American Express Refer a Friend programme. Where referral links are mentioned, please note: I only publish links for products I personally use. Amex referral links are unique to each cardholder — there is no single universal code. See the sections below for how to find or share your own link.

What is the American Express Refer a Friend scheme?

The American Express Refer a Friend programme is a UK loyalty initiative that allows existing Amex cardholders to invite friends, family members, or colleagues to apply for an eligible American Express card. When the referred person is approved and meets any applicable spending conditions, both the referrer and the new cardholder can earn bonus Membership Rewards points.

This is a UK-specific programme, distinct from American Express promotions in other markets. You may have seen references online to a welcome offer of 175,000 points — that figure relates to the US market and does not apply to UK Amex cards. UK welcome offers are set separately by American Express UK and are typically lower; always check the official Amex UK website for current UK figures.

How does the Amex referral programme work?

For the person referring (the referrer)

As an existing UK Amex cardholder, you have access to a unique personal referral link through your online account or the Amex app. Here is how to find and share it:

  1. Open the American Express app on your phone, or log in at americanexpress.com/en-gb.
  2. Navigate to Your Account or look under Useful Links on your dashboard.
  3. Select “Refer Friends. Earn Rewards.” (or the equivalent option shown in your account).
  4. Share your unique referral link via email, messaging, or by copying it directly.
  5. Once your referred friend is approved for a card and meets any qualifying spend conditions, you earn bonus Membership Rewards points.

Annual referral limits apply to how many friends you can refer and how many bonus points you can earn in a given year. Amex does not widely publicise the specific caps, and they are subject to change — check the official Amex UK Refer a Friend FAQ for the most current limits before you start referring.

For the person being referred (the referee)

If a friend sends you their personal Amex referral link, clicking it before you apply may unlock a boosted or enhanced welcome offer compared with applying through the standard Amex homepage. This is subject to availability and your own eligibility for the card in question.

To qualify for the referral bonus:

  • You must be new to the specific card you are applying for, or otherwise meet Amex’s eligibility criteria for referred applicants.
  • You will need to be approved for the card.
  • You must meet any qualifying spend conditions within the stated timeframe.

In some cases, Amex allows the referee to choose a different eligible card to the one held by the person referring them. Refer to the official Amex Refer a Friend FAQ for the current rules on card choice, as these are subject to change.

Which American Express cards are eligible?

The Refer a Friend programme applies across a range of UK Amex cards, including Membership Rewards-earning cards and cashback cards. However, eligibility can vary by card and by promotional period. Rather than publishing a list that may quickly become outdated, the most reliable source is the official American Express UK referral page, which is updated by Amex directly. Log in to your account to see which of your cards are currently participating in the scheme.

What bonus points can you earn?

Both the referrer and the referee can earn bonus Membership Rewards points as part of the scheme. The exact point amounts are not published here because they change regularly and vary by card. Fabricating or guessing these figures would not be useful to you.

Where to find current point amounts: Log in to your Amex account or app and navigate to the Refer a Friend section. The current offer for your specific card will be shown there. Alternatively, visit the official Amex UK Refer a Friend page.

Bonus points are typically awarded once the new cardholder has been approved and has met any qualifying spend conditions — points are not awarded at the point of application. Check the specific terms shown in your account for the timeline that applies to your card.

How to find your American Express referral code

Unlike some referral schemes that use a short alphanumeric code, Amex referrals work via a unique personal link generated for each cardholder. There is no single universal “Amex referral code” that everyone can share — your link is specific to you and your card account.

To retrieve your referral link:

  1. Via the app: Open the American Express app, go to ‘Account’ or ‘More’, and look for ‘Refer Friends. Earn Rewards.’
  2. Via desktop: Log in at americanexpress.com/en-gb, navigate to ‘Your Account’, and look under ‘Useful Links’ or the referral section of your dashboard.
  3. Copy your unique link or use the built-in share options (email, etc.) to send it to the person you wish to refer.
  4. Ask your friend to use your link when they start their Amex application — it must be used before they apply, not after.

Because each link is personal, you should never share your link publicly in a way that allows unknown third parties to use your referral quota. Amex’s terms set annual limits on how many referrals count towards your bonus — check those terms in your account.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my Amex referral code?

Log in to your American Express account online at americanexpress.com/en-gb or open the Amex app. Navigate to ‘Your Account’ and look for the ‘Refer Friends. Earn Rewards.’ option. Your unique personal referral link will be shown there, ready to copy or share.

Do Amex do referrals in the UK?

Yes. American Express operates an active Refer a Friend programme in the UK. Eligible existing cardholders can refer new applicants via a unique personal link, and both parties can earn bonus Membership Rewards points when the application is approved and any spend conditions are met.

Can my referred friend choose a different card to the one I hold?

In some cases, yes — Amex allows referred friends to apply for a different eligible card. The specific rules on card choice depend on the current programme terms. Check the official Amex UK Refer a Friend FAQ for the latest guidance, as this can change.

What bonus can I earn for referring a friend to Amex?

You earn bonus Membership Rewards points once your referred friend is approved and meets any qualifying spend conditions. The exact number of points varies by card and changes periodically — check the Refer a Friend section in your Amex app or online account for the current offer on your specific card. Is the 175,000-point Amex welcome offer available in the UK?

No. The 175,000-point figure is associated with US American Express promotions and does not apply to UK Amex cards. Welcome offers on UK Amex cards are set separately and are typically lower. Always check the official American Express UK website for the current UK welcome offer on any card you are considering.

Are there limits on how many friends I can refer?

Yes. Amex applies annual limits on the number of referrals that count towards your bonus and on the total bonus points you can earn per year through the scheme. The specific caps are not published in detail here as they are subject to change — check the Refer a Friend FAQ within your Amex account for the current limits that apply to your card.

Does using a referral link give my friend a better welcome offer?

Using a personal referral link may unlock an enhanced or boosted welcome offer for the new applicant, compared with applying directly through the standard Amex homepage. This is subject to availability and the applicant’s own eligibility. The referred applicant should check the offer shown on the application page before submitting.
