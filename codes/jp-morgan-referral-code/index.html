Frequently asked questions

Who can use the JP Morgan Personal Investing referral link?

The refer-a-friend offer is open to new UK customers who are aged 18 or over and do not already hold a JP Morgan Personal Investing account. Existing account holders are not eligible for a new-customer sign-up reward. JP Morgan Personal Investing reserves the right to withdraw or modify the offer at any time, so check the Mention Me landing page for the current eligibility criteria before you apply.

You can find the current minimum on the Mention Me landing page reached via my link above. How long does the reward take to arrive?

After you meet all the qualifying conditions (opening an eligible account, depositing the required amount, and holding it for the required period), your reward should be credited within [OFFER — confirm in app]. JP Morgan Personal Investing will typically notify you by email when the reward has been applied. If you believe you have qualified but have not received the reward, contact JP Morgan Personal Investing’s support team directly.

Is my money protected by the FSCS?

JP Morgan Personal Investing is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Cash held on the platform may be eligible for protection under the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) up to £85,000 per person, per institution. However, investments (funds, shares, ETFs) are not covered by the FSCS in the same way — FSCS protection for investments applies if the firm fails, not if the value of your investments falls. Always read JP Morgan Personal Investing’s own FSCS disclosure, available on their website, before investing.

What is JP Morgan Personal Investing, and is it the same as Nutmeg?

JP Morgan Personal Investing is a UK retail investing platform operated by J.P. Morgan Asset Management. It was built on the technology and infrastructure acquired when JP Morgan purchased Nutmeg in 2021. The platform allows UK investors to open a Stocks & Shares ISA, General Investment Account, or Junior ISA and choose from a range of managed and self-directed investment options. It is aimed at beginner-to-intermediate investors who want a straightforward digital experience backed by a large institution.
