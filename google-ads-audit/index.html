03 / The review

What I look at.

Six areas in the Full Audit, each judged on one question: is this spend earning its place? The Health Check covers the first three.

Wasted spend

The search terms, match types and placements that cost money and never convert.

Conversion tracking

Whether the numbers in Google Ads match GA4 and your real orders or enquiries. If the tracking is wrong, the bidding is chasing the wrong thing.

Account structure

Whether campaigns are organised so budget follows your margin and your priorities, not Google's defaults.

Performance Max and Shopping

Asset groups, feed quality, and how much of the reported revenue is brand traffic you would have won anyway.

Bidding and budgets

Whether the strategies and targets suit your volume, and where budget is capped in the wrong places.

Ads and landing pages

Whether the message matches the search, and whether the page gives the click a reason to act.

08 / Questions

The things people ask first.

What does it cost?

£750 for the Health Check, £1,250 for the Full Audit and £1,950 for the Audit Plus Support. Each is a fixed fee, confirmed in writing and paid before work starts. There is nothing to pay when you send the request.

Will you change anything in my account?

No. I ask for read-only access to Google Ads and GA4, which means I can see everything and edit nothing. Your campaigns keep running exactly as they are.

Will you try to sell me ongoing management?

No. The audit stands on its own and the report is yours to use however you like. If you want help putting it into practice afterwards, ask, and I will tell you plainly whether I have the capacity.

My agency controls the account. Can you still audit it?

Usually, yes. Even when the account sits inside an agency's own manager account, they can add a read-only user in a couple of minutes. If they will not, that is worth knowing in itself.

How long does it take?

Typically five working days from the point I have access. I take on a small number of audits each month, so I confirm the timing when I reply.
