Are hobbit houses real?

Yes, hobbit houses are real, and they're engineered buildings, not film sets. Ours are precast vaults set into the ground and finished the traditional way: natural stone, an oak door and a roof of grass and wildflowers.

Revonia has built more than 1,500 of them across Europe as homes, guest suites, studios, cellars and saunas. From the outside, turf and stone. Inside, curved ceilings, generous glazing and a stillness that above-ground buildings can't match.

Questions

Hobbit house questions, answered

Can you live in a hobbit house?

Yes. Our living ranges are fully insulated, heated, ventilated buildings with kitchen and bathroom options, built to be used every day of the year. The smaller cellar and sauna ranges are not living spaces, but everything from the Small Living range up is designed to be lived in.

How much does a hobbit house cost?

Turnkey pricing starts at £25,000 excl. VAT for a vaulted cellar and £45,000 for a liveable garden pod, rising to £199,000 for a 60–80m² underground family home. Prices include design, planning, groundworks, installation and fit-out. Final pricing follows a site assessment.

How long does a hobbit house take to build?

Where planning is already in place, 8–10 weeks from order to handover. Where we obtain planning permission for you first, around 6–7 months. The modules are built off-site, so time on your land is measured in days.

Do you need planning permission for a hobbit house?

Not always. In planning terms a smaller build is treated much like any underground garden room, and many fall within permitted development. Where permission is needed, we prepare and manage the application as part of the turnkey package, and it changes the timeline rather than the answer.

Are hobbit houses dark or damp?

No. Each vault is damp-proofed and ventilated as standard, and full-height glazed entrances and window options bring in far more daylight than most people expect. The result is a bright, dry interior with the quiet that only earth-sheltered buildings have.
